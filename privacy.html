PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy is important to us. Here is a straightforward explanation of what information we collect, why we need it, and how your details are kept safe.

Last Updated: September 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023
📋
Minimal Data

Only the basic details needed to book your room and assist your stay.

💳
No Card Storage

We never ask for or store credit cards, debit cards, or banking passwords.

🛡️
Zero Spam

Your contact information is never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.

⚡
Your Control

Request to view, update, or delete your booking details at any time.

1

What Information We Collect

When making a reservation enquiry or booking on our website, we only ask for the essentials:

  • Full Name: To identify you and prepare your room registration upon check-in.
  • Phone / WhatsApp: To send your booking confirmation, check-in instructions, and reach you for arrival assistance.
  • Email Address (Optional): To send your formal booking summary and digital receipt.
  • Stay Details: Check-in date, check-out date, number of guests, and room preference (AC or Non-AC).
  • Special Requests: Any optional preferences you share (e.g. arrival time, extra bedding).
💳
Payment Security Note: All room advance or balance payments are completed directly via official UPI QR or at our front desk upon arrival. Our website never requests or records financial credentials.
2

Why We Use Your Information

We process your information solely for legitimate hospitality services:

  • Room Allocation: Holding and reserving your chosen room category on your requested dates.
  • Guest Support: Answering questions via WhatsApp or phone call and coordinating check-in/check-out timings.
  • Legal Compliance: Maintaining lawful guest registry entries required under local tourist accommodation regulations.
3

How Your Information Is Protected

We employ modern security best practices to protect your data:

  • Encrypted Transmission: All submissions through our website are secured via industry-standard SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption.
  • Restricted Access: Only authorized Nandhanam Elite front-office personnel have access to guest reservation records.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, license, or provide your personal details to marketing firms or data brokers under any circumstances.
4

How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your booking records only as long as reasonably required to fulfill your stay and comply with statutory accounting and local guest register laws. Inactive records are systematically archived and cleared.

5

Your Rights & Choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, you have full control over your information:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the booking details we hold for you.
  • Right to Correction: You can ask us to update or correct any wrong contact details.
  • Right to Deletion: You can ask us to remove your booking record once your stay and statutory requirements are concluded.
6
