Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) Notice for Guests of Nandhanam Elite Tourist Home

Last Updated: August 2026 • Effective Date: August 2026

🏛️ 1. Data Fiduciary Information (DPDP §2 & §6)

Nandhanam Elite Tourist Home (“we”, “us”, or “our”), located at Kaithakod Junction, Vengalloor – Mangattukavala Bypass Road, Thodupuzha East PO, Pin: 685585, Kerala, India, acts as the Data Fiduciary responsible for the processing of your personal data when you interact with our website or submit a booking request.

📋 2. Categories of Personal Data Collected (DPDP §6)

In accordance with the principle of Data Minimisation, we only collect the minimum personal data required to arrange and confirm your accommodation:

Data Field Required / Optional Purpose
Full Name Required Guest identification and room reservation record.
Phone / WhatsApp Number Required Reservation confirmation, arrival coordination, and customer support.
Email Address Optional Digital booking receipt and reservation summary communication.
Dates & Guest Count Required Room availability allocation and calculation of accommodation charges.
Special Requests Optional Guest preferences (e.g., check-in timing, bedding assistance).

Note: We do not collect credit card, debit card, or net-banking credentials on this website. Payments are completed directly via official UPI/QR or on arrival.

🛡️ 3. Lawful Basis & Informed Consent (DPDP §4 & §6)

We process your personal data on the basis of your freely given, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent, provided when you check the consent confirmation box upon submitting a booking request.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken prior to such withdrawal.

☁️ 4. Data Processors & Cloud Safeguards (DPDP §8 & §16)

To provide real-time availability updates and maintain booking logs, reservation requests submitted on this website are transmitted via Google Apps Script to a secure Google Spreadsheet managed under Google Workspace (Google LLC) cloud infrastructure.

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access is strictly restricted to authorized property management personnel.
  • We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

⏳ 5. Retention Period (DPDP §8(7))

In accordance with Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act, personal data will be erased as soon as the purpose for which it was collected has been served, unless retention is necessary for compliance with applicable Indian laws (e.g., local tourist accommodation registers and tax/accounting regulations).

  • Active booking records are maintained until checkout completion.
  • Guest contact logs are archived and purged after 1 year unless required for regulatory verification.

⚖️ 6. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP §11–§14)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you enjoy the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access (§11): Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updating (§12): Request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (§12): Request deletion of your personal data once your stay is completed and no legal retention mandate applies.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (§13): Register any concerns or complaints regarding your personal data directly with our Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate (§14): Nominate an individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

👶 7. Processing of Children’s Data (DPDP §9)

Our booking form is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and above. When accompanying children stay at our property, details (e.g., child count) are provided directly by the parent or lawful guardian. We do not engage in tracking, behavioral profiling, or targeted communication directed at children.
